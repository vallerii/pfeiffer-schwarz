## Platform

web

## Users
Two equal primary audiences (confirmed):
- **Hotel owners and operators** planning a renovation of rooms, bathrooms, spa, lobby or the whole house — usually while the hotel stays open. Job: find one contractor who can run every trade without closing the hotel.
- **Private and commercial owners in Siegen-Wittgenstein** (flats, houses, offices, practices) needing single trades (tiles, painting, drywall, plaster/screed, sanitary) or a complete renovation.
Secondary: general contractors looking for a reliable subcontractor with full compliance documents; owners with water or fire damage (insurance cases).

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for PS Hotel- und Wohnbau GmbH ("Pfeiffer & Schwarz"), Bad Laasphe. It must route each visitor to the right path (hotel, building, bath in 3 days, damage) and turn them into an inquiry via the quiz or estimate forms. Success = qualified inquiries per path.

## Positioning
Around 40 of its own employed craftsmen (tilers, painters, drywallers, carpenters) instead of a subcontractor chain, and hotel renovation while the hotel keeps operating — one contact person from 3D plan to handover. (Confirmed.)
